This is a runbook for responding to ransomware incidents, aligned with the NIST SP 800-61 guidelines and the MITRE ATT&CK framework. The runbook is designed to be concise and easy to follow, with checkboxes to track progress during an active incident.

The runbook consists of four main sections:

1. **Preparation**: This section covers the necessary preparations for responding to a ransomware incident, including maintaining offline backups, documenting critical assets and owners, deploying and tuning EDR and logging tools, restricting lateral movement, blocking risky egress, training staff on phishing recognition, and drafting and approving the runbook.
2. **Detection & Analysis**: This section outlines the steps to be taken when detecting a ransomware incident, including confirming the alert, opening an incident ticket, identifying the scope of the incident, preserving evidence, collecting logs and network traffic data, correlating findings, classifying severity, determining the ransomware family, and mapping findings to MITRE ATT&CK techniques.
3. **Containment, Eradication & Recovery**: This section covers the steps to be taken to contain, eradicate, and recover from a ransomware incident, including isolating affected hosts, blocking known malicious IPs and domains, disabling compromised accounts and remote access, preventing spread, removing malware, rebuilding systems, patching exploited vulnerabilities, rotating secrets, and verifying eradication.
4. **Post-Incident Activity**: This section outlines the post-incident activities to be performed, including producing a final incident report, documenting indicators of compromise, conducting a lessons-learned meeting, updating controls, revising the runbook and training, completing regulatory and contractual notifications, and tracking remediation tasks.

The quick reference section provides key metrics to capture during an incident, such as time of first compromise, dwell time, C2 IP and beacon interval, first encrypted file timestamp, and accounts and hosts involved.

Overall, this runbook provides a comprehensive framework for responding to ransomware incidents, aligning with industry best practices and guidelines.
