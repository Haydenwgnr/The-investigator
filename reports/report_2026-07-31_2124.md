**Incident Report: Ransomware Attack**

### Summary

On May 2, 2026, at approximately 01:14:07 UTC, a ransomware attack was detected on our systems. The attacker gained access through brute-force login attempts and exploited vulnerabilities in our network. The malware encrypted critical files, rendering them inaccessible to users. This incident report details the timeline of events, root cause analysis, and recommended next actions for remediation.

### Root Cause

The attacker gained access through brute-force login attempts, specifically from the IP address 185.220.101.47. This IP address is associated with a known Command and Control (C2) server used by the ransomware family.

### Recommended Next Actions

1. **Remediation**:
	* Isolate affected hosts from the network (disable NIC / move to quarantine VLAN)
	* Block known malicious IPs/domains at firewall and DNS
	* Disable compromised accounts and reset credentials for privileged users
	* Remove malware, backdoors, and unauthorized tools from all identified hosts
2. **Investigation**:
	* Collect additional auth logs — look for brute force, SUCCESS LOGIN from unusual IPs
	* Collect network logs — look for C2 beaconing (regular intervals, fixed payload size)
3. **Communication**:
	* Notify leadership and stakeholders of the incident
	* Provide a final incident report: timeline, root cause, impact, and actions taken

### Post-Incident Activity

1. Produce a final incident report
2. Document indicators of compromise (IOCs) and share with ISACs / threat intel feeds
3. Conduct a lessons-learned meeting within 5 business days of closure
4. Update controls: firewall rules, MFA enforcement, backup strategy, detection rules
